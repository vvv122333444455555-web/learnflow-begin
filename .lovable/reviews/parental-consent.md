# Phase 1: parental consent and existing accounts

Existing child accounts, school names, phone numbers, progress and subscriptions are retained. No existing account is silently reassigned, downgraded or deleted.

New kids-product registration is for a parent, using the parent's email. It does not ask a child for an email, school or phone. Creating and switching between email-free managed child profiles is a separate parent-workspace feature and is not introduced in Phase 1.

Existing parent-child links must be established by an administrator after verifying the adult's authority over the child. A child cannot claim a parent, change a link, impersonate a parent or move itself to the adult track. No automatic backfill guesses family relationships.

Before enabling child AI or paid services, the parent must receive a versioned notice describing purposes, minimum data, AI provider processing/retention, costs, access controls and withdrawal. Consent must be attributable to the verified linked parent, timestamped and revocable; neither a registration checkbox nor a child's self-declaration is adequate. No consent is assumed for existing accounts.

Phase 1 keeps all kids-track AI disabled (including parent accounts using the shared kids workspace). No child text is sent to an AI provider or saved by the new assistant. Existing stored messages are retained but hidden from child accounts. Guided curriculum interactions, output moderation, verified consent collection and usage limits are prerequisites, not claims of completed features.

Checkout is disabled for everyone until a real provider verifies successful payments and trusted server-side prices/durations are implemented. Existing paid access is preserved.
